THE TABLE RULES

How to play

  1. A coin flip decides who chooses the first chosen shape. See your five cards before choosing.
  2. If you picked the shape, select two cards to keep. Otherwise, select three. The other cards are put aside.
  3. Take turns building a 13-card hand. Keep the offered card (and see the next card discarded), or pass and take the next card unseen.
  4. The person who picked the shape plays first. Match the shape of the first card if you can; otherwise play any card. The chosen shape beats other shapes. Card order from lowest to highest: 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, Jack (J), Queen (Q), King (K), Ace (A). Ace is the highest card.
  5. The higher card of the shape played first wins unless a card of the chosen shape is played. Each pair winner plays first next. First to 7 card pairs wins!
  6. Tap Play again for a new round. The winner chooses the next chosen shape.

Your opponent cannot see your cards.
